The network law: pods in eval-sandbox may egress only to DNS, artifactory:5000, and OpenAI (:443) — they cannot reach any victim/SaaS/internal service directly. artifactory (root process, bypassing the in-pod iptables that confine the agent) is the one hop allowed to fan out via /github-remote?url=<target> — but only to cybergym and pastebin; it cannot reach hub or dataset-worker. Those are reached by chaining egress: cybergym → hub → dataset-worker (HWK ingress is app: hub only). Once SSTI-executed, dataset-worker (HWK) becomes the deepest pivot, reaching the k8s API, IMDS, Mongo, the Headscale mesh, metrics, and GitHub Enterprise.
